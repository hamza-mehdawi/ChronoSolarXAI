# ChronoSolarXAI: Explainable Feature-Engineered Machine Learning for Long-Term Photovoltaic Energy Forecasting

**ChronoSolarXAI: Explainable Feature-Engineered Machine Learning for Long-Term Photovoltaic Energy Forecasting** is an end-to-end machine learning and Explainable AI (XAI) framework developed for long-term photovoltaic (PV) solar energy forecasting. By integrating historical meteorological measurements, domain-driven feature engineering, seasonal dynamics, and post-hoc interpretability techniques (SHAP/LIME), the project delivers accurate solar energy predictions alongside transparent, physically consistent feature attributions.

## 🔍 Overview

Reliable long-term forecasting of photovoltaic energy generation is essential for grid stability, energy storage scheduling, and renewable energy integration. While modern ensemble and deep learning architectures offer strong predictive accuracy, their black-box behavior limits operational trust. **ChronoSolarXAI** addresses this challenge through three core stages:

1. **Data Preprocessing & Seasonal Engineering:** Cleaning raw meteorological and PV records and extracting cyclical, temporal, and seasonal features.
2. **Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA):** Analyzing feature distributions, missingness, seasonal shifts, and correlation structures.
3. **Feature-Engineered Forecasting & Explainability (XAI):** Benchmarking predictive models and applying SHAP/LIME to quantify how engineered, meteorological, and temporal variables drive PV energy output.

## 📊 Datasets

1. **`data/energy_weather_data.csv`** (Raw Input):
   * Historical timestamps, photovoltaic energy generation measurements, and meteorological features (e.g., solar irradiance, ambient/module temperature, relative humidity, wind speed, and cloud cover).

2. **`data/seasonsdata_cleaned.csv`** (Processed Output):
   * Generated by the preprocessing pipeline after anomaly filtering, missing value handling, timestamp alignment, and seasonal feature encoding.

## 🧠 Methodology

* **Preprocessing & Imputation:** Nighttime zero-irradiance alignment, outlier removal, and temporal interpolation.
* **Feature & Seasonal Engineering:** Cyclical transformations ($\sin/\cos$ encodings for hour of day and day of year), lagged/rolling weather features, and seasonal indicators.
* **Correlation & Statistical Analysis:** Multi-season correlation profiling to identify collinear meteorological predictors and thermal derating effects.
* **Explainable AI (XAI):** Global and local SHAP value attribution to verify model alignment with physical photovoltaic behavior.
